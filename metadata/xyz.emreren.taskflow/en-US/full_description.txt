TaskFlow is a lightweight, local-first task manager. All data stays on your
device: there is no network access, no analytics, and no advertising.

Features:

* Multiple local profiles, each with its own password-protected task list.
* Custom task groups with icons to organize your tasks.
* Light and dark themes.
* Fully offline: your tasks never leave your device.

TaskFlow does not require an internet connection and does not collect or
transmit any personal data.
